You Have Found "The Medusa Hologram"!

Memory Holder: Mark Holzbach, Spatial Imaging, M.S. ‘86

This was a project that encapsulates and embodies the boundary crossing collaboration spirit of the early media lab. Members of disparate research groups (myself, David Chen, and Alejandro Ferdman) came together and used their specialized knowledge and expertise to create this unique (and I think quite iconic) early Media Lab art work. A large copy of this hologram was displayed at the MIT Museum last year as part of their featured "Optiker" Stephen Benton exhibition (https://mitmuseum.mit.edu/exhibitions/optiker). The human model for the Medusa was fellow grad student Betsy Connors (who later married David Chen). Although we produced a number of smaller version holograms in the basement holography lab in E14, it was fellow holographer Craig Newswanger who produced this large version copy on a unique specialized large-scale holographic stereogram machine he built in the lab at Advanced Dimensional Displays. Alejandro Ferdman, Michael Klug (not involved in the Medusa), and I later went on to co-found digital holography company Zebra Imaging (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebra_Imaging) that operated successfully for about 20 years (closing in 2016). Many MIT Media Lab and Zebra Imaging holograms are in the collection of the MIT Museum as is the large hologram collection belonging to the late Stephen Benton.
