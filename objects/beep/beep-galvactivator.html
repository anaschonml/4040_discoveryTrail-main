You Have Found "The Galvactivator"!

Memory Holder: Jocelyn Scheirer, Affective Computing

The Galvactivator was the first wearable washable sensor! It measures skin conductance and maps it to a glowing LED on the back of the glove. 2000 were manufactured, with the help of Phillips Design, in 1999. They were given out to an audience in Kresge auditorium at the *SENS*BLES* symposium. Patented in 2002. Many of them still work after 30 years! Was recently acquired by the Science Museum Group, UK.
