You Have Found "Audio Spotlight"

Memory Holder: Joe Pompei, Music, Mind and Machine, PhD ‘02

Audio Spotlight began as my doctoral research at the MIT Media Lab, based on the idea that sound didn’t have to spread everywhere like it does from a conventional loudspeaker. Instead, it uses a narrow beam of ultrasound to create audible sound in a very specific area, almost like shining a spotlight of sound. The work eventually became the foundation of Holosonics and the commercial Audio Spotlight technology now in use all over the world.

The Media Lab was the only lab in the world willing to take a chance on my idea, and remarkably, it did so precisely because the experts thought it was impossible. Nicholas Negroponte heard about the idea and ran it by a number of professors, including Amar Bose, and they universally panned it. Nicholas’s reaction was, “This is exactly the kind of student we want. Bring him in!” Six months later, I had a fully working prototype.
