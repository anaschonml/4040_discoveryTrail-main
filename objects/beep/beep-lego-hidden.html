You Have Found "Hidden LEGO Pieces"!

Memory Holder: Michelle Hlubinka, Lifelong Kindergarten, M.S. ‘03

For MindFest we would pull out the extra LEGO, which we kept in bags under the floor panels in the Cube. Nobody could ever remember which panels held the stash, and it required a special tool to open the panels, so it was always a bit of an adventure to find them. Some of the pieces were really weird, and we had like 2000 of them.
