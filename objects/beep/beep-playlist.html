You Have Found "the early media lab music playlist!"

Memory Holder: Mark Holzbach, Spatial Imaging, M.S. ‘86

I think many alumni would feel energized to reminisce about the music they listened to while working at the Media Lab. The mostly nocturnal-by-necessity members of my holography lab (SPI or Spatial Imaging Group) often listened to minimal music by musicians like Steve Reich, Kraftwerk, and Philip Glass. We were nocturnal because the night was when undesired building vibrations were at a minimum (stable conditions are required for recording holograms). The Computer Garden music playlist was perhaps more eclectic than the SPI. It often included Talking Heads (who Walter Bender collaborated with on album covers), Peter Gabriel, Natalie Merchant, The Art of Noise, the Smiths, the violent femmes, and many others (just ask any old media lab Alum!). - it might be a very cool project to maintain a web page on which ML alumni could propose (and maybe even vote on) their favorites sorted by year / era.
